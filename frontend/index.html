How to Play

  1. Click Start Webcam and allow camera access.
  2. Hold up a Rock ✊ so the game knows you're ready.
  3. Shake your rock up and down to begin the "Scissors, Paper, Rock!" countdown.
  4. On the final beat ("Rock!"), show your real move — Rock, Paper, or Scissors.
  5. Your detected move appears over your camera feed, the AI's move in its box — then see who won!

No webcam handy? Use the Quick Play buttons instead.

AI Modes

  • 🧠 AI Predictive — blends frequency + Markov prediction with anti-counter play to exploit patterns in your moves.
  • 🎲 AI Random — plays uniformly at random (the game-theoretic Nash equilibrium — unbeatable in theory).
